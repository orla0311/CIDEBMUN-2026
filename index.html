01 — The conference

A bilingual floor for real diplomacy

CIDEBMUN is the Model United Nations conference of the Centro de Investigación y Desarrollo Educativo Bilingüe in Monterrey. Delegates research a country or character, draft position papers, negotiate blocs and pass resolutions under formal parliamentary procedure — in English or in Spanish, depending on the committee.

Committees are graded Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced, so a first-time delegate and a returning chair both find a room that fits. Alongside the standard General Assembly and council formats, the conference runs crisis, special and joint committees where the agenda changes while you debate it.

Formats
Regular · Crisis · Special · Joint

Regular committees follow standard rules of procedure. Crisis committees respond to live updates from the crisis staff. Special and joint committees break the mould entirely.

Levels
Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced

Four beginner committees, three intermediate, and three advanced rooms for delegates who have chaired or won awards before.

Languages
English & Spanish

Most committees sit in English. The Human Rights Council and the Security Council debate in Spanish, and their topics are published in Spanish.

02 — Committees & topics

Eleven rooms, twenty topics

Each committee debates two topics and seats twenty delegates. Every committee has its own page: topics in full, the letter from its dais, its chairs and the background paper submission.

04 — Delegates

How to take a seat

01

Choose a committee

Pick a level and a working language. Delegations may request specific rooms; single delegates are placed by the Chief of Committees.

02

Register and pay

Registration and payment details are issued by the Chief of Finances once your school confirms its delegation size.

03

Prepare

Study guides, rules of procedure and position paper templates are published for every committee ahead of the conference.

04

Debate

Formal debate, moderated caucus, working papers, amendments, vote. Awards are given per committee.
